Where each one stands

The desk closed #4471 on 5 October with an engineering update:

  • Parser (ticket, closed): fixed, “fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales”.
  • Form hint (ticket, closed): won’t fix, “booking-v3 form retired”.
  • One day early (ticket, open): not in the update. Reading dates day first does not change a date that moves by one day, so these reports stay open.

How to tell them apart

  • Parser: a day-first tenant’s date is read month first, so day and month trade places. Any channel: app, email, and the form on tenants that do not run booking-v3.
  • Form hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, and people type what it says. Only through the form, only on booking-v3 tenants. The bodies read like parser reports; the channel and the form tell them apart.
  • One day early: the weekday is off by one, or two time zones see two days. No day and month swap. The desk already had these as one-day moves in desk-notes.json.

How this was counted
